# Community code of conduct

Naija Support Kit welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels
of experience. Discuss the work respectfully and help others participate.

## Expected behavior

- Give constructive feedback and explain technical disagreements.
- Respect others' time, identity and experience.
- Ask before sharing private conversations or personal information.
- Keep examples synthetic and protect customer information and credentials.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, impersonation, spam and deliberate
disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

## Moderation and reporting

The maintainer, [@ogeobubu](https://github.com/ogeobubu), may edit or hide harmful
content, close off-topic discussions or restrict participation to protect the
community. Repeated or serious violations may lead to exclusion from project
spaces.

Use GitHub's report-content controls or
[GitHub's abuse reporting channel](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse)
for abusive behavior. Avoid reposting harmful content or private details in a
public issue. Report software vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
