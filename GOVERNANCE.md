# Project governance

Naija Support Kit is an independently maintained open-source product led by
Oge Obubu, [@ogeobubu](https://github.com/ogeobubu). Its public repository lives
under that personal GitHub account, with a product identity and contribution
process managed by its maintainer.

## Maintainer responsibilities

The maintainer controls repository settings, reviews and merges changes,
prioritizes the roadmap and decides when to publish releases. Public contribution
does not automatically grant repository write access or production access.

Use issues to propose work and pull requests to review implementation. The
CODEOWNERS file identifies the maintainer for review; it does not by itself
establish branch-protection requirements.

## Open source and services

The MIT license permits use and modification of this repository, including
commercial use, subject to its terms. Hosting, configuration, training and
maintenance may be offered as separate paid services. A service agreement does
not change the license of the public code.

Customer records, production credentials, pricing negotiations and prospect
tracking belong in private systems. Only reusable software, synthetic examples
and general documentation belong in this repository.
