# Retailer pilot onboarding

Use this checklist with one Nigerian retailer before configuring its assistant.
The owner must approve the answers and nominate a staff member for handoffs.
Keep the fictional demo separate until the business details are complete.

## Choose the first business

Start with a clothing, beauty or household goods seller you can contact directly.
Choose someone who receives repeated WhatsApp questions, has a current product
list and can respond to escalations during agreed hours.

| Detail | Owner response |
| --- | --- |
| Business name and product category | To supply |
| Owner or authorised contact | To supply |
| Business WhatsApp number | To supply |
| Approximate customer messages per day | To supply |
| Current person handling enquiries | To supply |
| Main problem to improve | To supply |
| Staff member receiving escalations | To supply |
| Staff coverage hours in WAT | To supply |
| Approved fallback outside staff hours | To supply |
| Pilot start and review dates | To agree after the demo works |

Ask the owner for common questions with names, phone numbers, addresses and
payment details removed. Never ask for passwords or tokens in this questionnaire.
The owner connects the business account through the appropriate setup flow.

## Collect twenty approved answers

Write the exact customer-facing answer in the last column. An unanswered question
must go to staff. Confirm which information changes frequently and who updates it.

| 2 | Where can I view the current catalogue? | To supply |
| 3 | What are your opening hours? | To supply |
| 4 | Do you open on Sundays or public holidays? | To supply |
| 5 | Where is your shop or pickup location? | To supply |

For this pilot, staff handle payment confirmation, refunds, complaints, current
stock and order-specific questions even when a general policy is documented.
An FAQ answer must never imply that a payment, refund or order has been approved.

## Agree the handoff process

- Staff monitor the open and unassigned Chatwoot inbox during the agreed hours.
- Customers can request a person at any point.
- The assistant gives no promised response time until the owner approves one.
- Staff take over an escalated conversation; automation stays paused.
- Staff return a conversation to pending only when they want the bot to resume.
- Identify a backup contact for the primary staff member's absence.

## Approve the pilot launch

- [ ] Replace the fictional answers in `knowledge/business.json`.
- [ ] Owner checks all prices, delivery areas, hours and policies.
- [ ] Confirm the assistant introduces itself as automated.
- [ ] Test the owner's actual questions and expected answers.
- [ ] Test staff takeover, complaints, unknown questions and attachments.
- [ ] Confirm the business owns its WhatsApp number and Meta account.
- [ ] Agree who may access customer conversations and when records are deleted.
- [ ] Agree the pilot scope, fees, variable costs and staff coverage.
- [ ] Confirm the website demo works before connecting a production number.
- [ ] Record owner approval and the date; schedule the first review.

## Review after fourteen days of live use

Record customer messages received, sampled answer accuracy, escalations,
staff time spent, direct operating costs and owner feedback. Compare staff effort
with the baseline collected before the pilot. Ask whether the owner will renew
at the agreed monthly price and which unanswered questions are worth adding next.
